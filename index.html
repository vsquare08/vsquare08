About

I like building things that make learning feel less like a chore and more like a conversation. My first big project was a chatbot that listens, summarizes, and quizzes you, a study buddy that never gets tired of repeating the same topic.

Right now I'm exploring AI in healthcare, mostly brain and neuro applications: models that could help with early detection, diagnosis support, or personalized neuro-rehabilitation.

I read neuro papers at night and regret my sleep schedule in the morning.

  • AI that actually helps people learn, not demos that only look good in a pitch deck.
  • Reliable AI for healthcare, where a wrong output isn't just a bug, it's a risk.
  • Clean code, clean architecture, and projects I'd actually use myself.
  • The spot where NLP meets real-world UX.
