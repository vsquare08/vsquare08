### 🎯 What I'm working on

Right now I'm exploring **AI + Healthcare**, mostly **brain and neuro applications**: models that could help with early detection, diagnosis support, or personalized neuro-rehabilitation.

Before that I built an AI chatbot that listens, summarizes, and quizzes you. A study buddy that never gets tired of repeating the same topic.

### 🧠 What I care about

- AI that actually helps people learn, not demos that only look good in a pitch deck
- Reliable AI for healthcare, where a wrong output isn't just a bug, it's a risk
- Clean code, clean architecture, and projects I'd actually use myself
- The spot where NLP meets real-world UX
